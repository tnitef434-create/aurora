# Aurora 1.7.2

## Relic powers
- **At most 2 relics per run**, and only relics from the chapter you're playing can be used.
- A relic you **just found** can be spent right away. You no longer have to finish the level first.
- Picking up a relic now tells you how to use it: **Touchpad / Tab → Powers**.
- The **path** is now a glowing ribbon on the ground with arrows flowing toward your goal, sparkles drifting along it and a pulsing ring at the target.
- The Powers list scrolls properly, and the header showing your uses left stays visible.

## Polish
- **New medals:** gold, silver and bronze now look like real medals, with a ribbon, milled rim, embossed star and a metallic sheen. They swing on the level complete screen.
- **Dash introduction:** a cleaner, smaller card with a tiny animated demo and three quick facts.
- **Settings:** with a controller or keyboard you can now scroll all the way down. The last rows no longer sit in the blurred edge.
