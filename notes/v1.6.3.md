# Aurora 1.6.3

The last update before **1.7 and Chapter 2**.

## Play menu
- **Redesigned chapter select:** bigger chapter cards with aurora art, a progress bar, and one pip per level showing your medal. Chapter 2 shows **Coming in 1.7**.
- **Level select:** all 7 levels fit on one screen. The selected level shows its description, par, best time, medal and relics. The start button now says which level you're starting.

## Polish
- The top glow bar now grows out from the center exactly as a page finishes opening, and disappears the moment you close it.
- **Collection** now unfolds smoothly from its tab. The tab stays lit while it's open.
