# Aurora 1.6

## New
- **Chapters.** Press Play to pick a chapter first. All 7 levels are now **Chapter 1: The Aurora**. **Chapter 2 is coming soon.**
- **Apply button for settings.** Settings no longer save automatically. Press **Apply** to keep your changes; leaving without applying undoes them.
- **Controller Prompts setting.** Turn off PlayStation button icons if you only use keyboard and mouse (Settings → General).

## Changes
- **Collection** is no longer in the main menu list. Open it from the **Collection** tab at the top.

## Fixes
- The glowing bar at the top of a settings page now starts in the middle instead of sliding in from the right.
