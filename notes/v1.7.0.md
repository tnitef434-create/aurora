# Aurora 1.7: Chapter 2, The Wilds

## New: Chapter 2
- **A living island floating under the stars.** Chapter 2 leaves the crystal void for a hand-built jungle island: grassy paths, a forest all around, a waterfall pouring off the edge, and smaller islands drifting through space.
- **Level 1: Whispering Grove.** Trees make up the maze walls. Thorn traps hide in the grass.
- **Insects:** a new enemy that thinks for itself. They wander their own part of the maze, notice you when they can see you, chase you along the paths, then hover, lock on and lunge. Break line of sight or jump the lunge to escape.
- **An explorer robot:** your robot gets a nature look for the island, with leaves, vines and a flower on the backpack.
- **2 new relics:** *Amber Heartseed* and *Moonleaf Totem*.
- Trees between the camera and you sink out of the way so they never hide you.

## Changes
- Chapter select now lists both chapters, and levels are numbered within their chapter.
- Chapter cards have animated backgrounds.

## Fixes
- The level details above the Start button were stuck faded out. They're fully visible now.
