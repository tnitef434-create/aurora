# Aurora 1.7.1

## New
- **Dash.** Some maps now let you dash forward: press **F** or **○**. A dash uses stamina and takes 1 second to recharge. The stamina bar sits under your hearts. Dash is on in **Aurora's Heart** (Chapter 1) and **Whispering Grove** (Chapter 2). These maps show a dash icon in level select, and they explain the dash the first time you load them.
- **Relic powers.** In a level, press **Touchpad / Tab** and open the new **Powers** tab. Spend a relic you've collected to light a glowing trail on the ground to your next goal for 1 minute. The relic leaves your Collection until you find it again.
- **Relics move every run.** Hidden relics now spawn in a different spot each time you play a level.
- **Start Countdown setting** (Settings → General) for speedrunners: a 3-2-1-GO before each run, and the timer starts on GO.
- **Nature music** for Chapter 2: kalimba, warm pads, birdsong, crickets and wind.

## Improved
- **Insects** now fly facing forward, and they have smoother, cleaner bodies with two pairs of wings. They lean into turns, pump their abdomen, curl their sting before they lunge and tuck their legs while diving.
