# Aurora 1.5: Level Impossible

**The final update of Chapter 1.**

## New: Level 7, Level Impossible
- **The biggest map yet:** 25×25 tiles, up from 19×19 in Aurora's Heart.
- **Hazards everywhere:** 11 rotating lasers, 16 spike traps, 6 patrol drones and 11 crumbling tiles, plus a moving platform, a bounce-pad jump over the void, and gaps to jump.
- **Genuinely hard:** lasers and drones move 25% faster, you only get **2 hearts** on any difficulty, and par is 6:00.
- A new blood-red and ember palette, and its own music.
- **2 new relics:** *Shard of the Impossible* and *Crown of Chapter One*, bringing the total to 14.
- Clearing it ends Chapter 1 with a new "Chapter 1 complete" screen in all 6 languages.

## Other changes
- **Play in your browser with no install:** the game now ships prebuilt, so you can just open `index.html`. It also works on GitHub Pages.
- The Collection and Records screens show the new relics.

## Download
Get **Aurora-win64.zip** below, unzip it and run `Aurora.exe`. If Windows SmartScreen appears, click **More info → Run anyway**.
